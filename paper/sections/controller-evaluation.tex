\paragraph{Completion and rejection.}
The Adaptive controller completed \ControllerCompleted{} of
\ControllerPairs{} input pairs, compared with \FixedCompleted{} for Fixed.
It rejected \ControllerBudgetRejected{} pairs after exhausting the ladder.
These counts describe execution under the frozen self-check rules.
A rejection does not establish that no configuration can meet the offline
quality constraint; a completed panorama does not establish that the
constraint was met. The all-pair latency distribution retains both outcomes.

\paragraph{Reading the geometry comparison.}
The two methods retain different homography populations. Their separate
median MCE values therefore combine estimation error with selection.
The paired comparison uses \GeometryJointPairs{} inputs retained by both,
while reporting completion and rejection over the entire cohort.
The error curve also retains the full cohort in its denominator, so rejected
pairs cannot make the displayed fraction of accurately registered pairs larger.
Neither a subset mean nor a paired mean establishes geometric noninferiority
for inputs the controller rejected.
