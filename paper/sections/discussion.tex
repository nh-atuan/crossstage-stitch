\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}
% Role: concrete threats and the evidence needed to resolve them.
A global homography cannot model arbitrary parallax or independent motion.
Reference-relative QoR depends on the reliability of the high-budget fit.
The overlap policy measures alignment, while A4's crops describe composition
qualitatively.

The measured heuristic reduces latency on its joint-pass subset while losing
QoR coverage over the held-out cohort. The T4 results therefore do not establish
the full-cohort runtime hypothesis. Self-check feedback can accept a model
that fails the offline alignment predicate. Conditional speedup and unconditional
pass rate must be reported together. Later learned or pooled policies are
separate configurations whose results require their own frozen evidence.

Synthetic end-to-end failures limit conclusions drawn from renderer throughput.
Known-homography rendering bypasses registration. A large corner error can
coexist with successful execution. Cross-architecture measurements are pending.
