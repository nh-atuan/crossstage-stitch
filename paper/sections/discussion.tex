\section{Limitations and Next Evidence}
\label{sec:discussion}
% Role: concrete threats and the evidence needed to resolve them.
A single global homography cannot represent arbitrary parallax or independent
object motion. Relative overlap quality can also mislead when the high-budget
reference is unreliable. The current policy addresses alignment over a fixed
domain; seam quality and perceptual composition need separate evaluation.
Multi-band depth changes filtering cost and image content but is not assumed
to improve quality monotonically.

The research question remains open until an adaptive controller is compared
with fixed budgets under the same frozen policy. A development oracle gap alone
does not account for feature cost, false acceptance, retry, fallback, or
invalidated work. If those costs remove the expected benefit, the negative
result should delimit when adaptation is useful rather than be hidden by
reporting only fast successful cases. Hardware conclusions require the stated
T4 environment; local checks retain their own device identity. The final
conclusion will be written from those measurements.
