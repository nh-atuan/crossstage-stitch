Table~\ref{tab:s2-s3-a3} times S2 and S3 alone on the 50 images
($512\times512$) of the 25 pilot pairs from the UDIS-D training split, with 2
warmups and 10 repeats per image and budget. S2 time depends mainly on the
image size; at $S=1$ it grows only from 0.077 to 0.087\,ms across all budgets.
S3 time follows the number of keypoints kept: 0.057\,ms for 500 and
0.122--0.123\,ms for 1801.5. These images run out of corners early. At
$S=0.25$ no image yields 500 keypoints, and at $S=0.5$ the median stays at 582
keypoints for every budget from 1000 up. At a small scale, raising $K$ costs
little time and adds few features. CUDA events placed between kernels changed
the stage totals by up to 11\%, so we use them only for shares. At $S=1$, the
ranking kernel takes 18\% of S2 at $K=500$ and about 27\% at $K\ge1000$, and
the descriptor kernel takes 70--86\% of S3.

Allocating once for the largest budget is cheap when each attempt passes a view
of capacity $K$. A view of the full allocation slows S3 by 0.007\,ms at
$K=1000$ and by at most 0.001\,ms at the other budgets. Reallocating every
attempt adds 0.030--0.047\,ms per image for $S\le0.75$ and 0.173--0.193\,ms at
$S=1$. Over 4000 consecutive attempts per mode, it also raised the p95 of host
time from 0.24 to about 0.40\,ms in each of three runs.

In the full pipeline on the 25 pairs at $S=0.5$, $R=256$, and feather blending,
S2 and S3 together take about 0.19\,ms of a median end-to-end time of 3.17\,ms
at $K=500$ and 3.50\,ms at $K=1000$. A larger budget costs time mainly in S4
and S5 on pairs with many keypoints: the S4 median rises from 0.186 to
0.259\,ms, and its p95 from 0.187 to 1.184\,ms at $K=2000$.
