\subsection{Held-Out Quality and Latency}
\label{sec:held-out}
The nominal UDIS-D cohort retains \ChNominal{} pairs in each mode, including
\ChLoaderFailed{} loader failure. The finite Oracle grid contains \ChGrid{}
configurations. Fixed-Best was chosen on development and coincides with
Fixed-High at $s=\ChFixedS$, $k=\ChFixedK$, $r=\ChFixedR$ with feather blending. A failed
reference makes \ChReferenceFailed{} test pairs unverified under the relative
policy. Their dispositions remain in the denominator.

\input{tables/table_ch02_summary}
Fixed-Best and Oracle each pass on \ChFixedPass{} pairs
(\ChFixedPercent{}\%). Adaptive passes on \ChAdaptivePass{}
(\ChAdaptivePercent{}\%). A successful invocation can therefore return a
panorama that fails the offline alignment predicate. Agreement with the
high-budget reference measures relative alignment and does not establish
independent ground-truth accuracy.

Fixed-Best/Adaptive latency ratio is \ChSpeedup{}$\times$. The corresponding

Stage timing on the common passing pairs places the largest observed
differences in matching and robust fitting. The artifact breakdown reports
individual stage medians and the host total as separate measurements. GPU
intervals and host waits can overlap, and stage medians need not sum to the
host median.
