\subsection{Resolution Scaling and Registration Limits}
\label{sec:scaling}
Synthetic inputs use controlled affine and projective transformations at
720p, 1080p and 4K. Half-pixel coordinate scaling transforms the ground-truth
homography between resolutions. The S6-only track supplies that homography
directly. The end-to-end track estimates its own homography and evaluates
geometric error against the known transform.

S6-only completes \ChRenderSuccess{}/\ChRenderTotal{} pair--blender cases.
Its 4K canvases span \ChCanvasMin{}--\ChCanvasMax{} pixels in width, exceeding

Workspace query, declared allocation and peak VRAM are separate quantities.

Registration is the limiting factor in the separate end-to-end track. Only
\ChEndSuccess{}/\ChEndTotal{} pair--blender cases complete. The largest
repeat median of mean corner error is \ChGeometryError{} pixels. All failures
remain in the ledger. These observations establish S6 render scaling with
known geometry and a failure of the tested registration configuration on
most synthetic stress cases.

\subsection{Cross-Architecture Performance (L4 and A100)}
\label{sec:cross-arch}
\input{tables/table_cross_architecture}
To evaluate architectural generalizability, the 54 known-geometry S6 cases
were re-executed on NVIDIA L4 (\texttt{sm\_89}, Ada Lovelace) and NVIDIA
A100-SXM4 (\texttt{sm\_80}, Ampere) under the identical synthetic manifest and
seed (\texttt{20261030}).
All 54 cases complete without failure on both architectures ($54/54$).
As shown in Table~\ref{tab:cross-architecture}, L4 delivers a consistent
$1.77\times$--$1.90\times$ speedup over T4 across all resolutions and blend
modes.
Its $48$\,MB L2 cache effectively amortizes intermediate pyramid traffic: at
4K, extending multi-band from 3 to 5 levels incurs less than $0.06\%$
additional latency ($56.610$\,ms vs.\ $56.641$\,ms), compared to a $0.94$\,ms
gap on T4.

On A100, the $2{,}039$\,GB/s HBM2e bus provides a $33.35\times$--$35.58\times$
speedup for streaming Feather blending ($0.751$\,ms at 4K).
For multi-band, A100's speedup over T4 scales from $2.65\times$--$2.81\times$ at
720p to $4.31\times$--$4.36\times$ at 4K ($22.966$\,ms for MB3), reflecting
improved SM saturation on large pyramid tiles.
Crucially, maximum queried workspace matches bitwise across all three
architectures, verifying that memory sizing is determined strictly by canvas
geometry and remains invariant to GPU microarchitecture.
