\subsection{Resolution Scaling and Registration Limits}
\label{sec:scaling}
Synthetic inputs use controlled affine and projective transformations at
720p, 1080p and 4K. Half-pixel coordinate scaling transforms the ground-truth
homography between resolutions. The S6-only track supplies that homography
directly. The end-to-end track estimates its own homography and evaluates
geometric error against the known transform.
